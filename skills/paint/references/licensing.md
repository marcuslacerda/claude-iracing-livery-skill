# Licensing — what you may and may not do with the files

- **This plugin** (code, docs, calibrations) is MIT, not affiliated with iRacing.com Motorsport
  Simulations, Trading Paints or any car manufacturer.
- **The PSD templates are iRacing's copyrighted material.** The user downloads them from
  iracing.com/painttextures with their own membership and keeps them in `<workspace>/templates/`.
  Never copy, upload, attach or share a template or the layers exported from it; never commit them to
  a repository; never suggest another download source. The repo's `.gitignore` already excludes them.
- **`islands.json`, `islands_map.png` and `panels.md`** in `cars/` are calibration data (coordinates,
  numbers, notes) derived from the templates: a reference, not a usable template. They are shared for
  calibration and would be removed on iRacing's request.
- **Logos and trademarks**: the user's own logo is theirs. Anyone else's (a bank, a brand, a team,
  a series) is a trademark: painting it is the user's responsibility — fine for a personal proposal
  or with permission, not for an unauthorised "sponsor" look shared publicly. iRacing's sporting
  code and Trading Paints' terms forbid offensive content and unauthorised marks. Never add a
  third-party logo to the plugin's public designs or examples.
- **Fonts**: the engine uses Windows system fonts in place (Bahnschrift, Segoe UI, Arial, Consolas)
  and never copies them. Custom fonts go in `<workspace>/fonts/`, under the font's own licence.
- **The TGAs rendered belong to the user**; what they upload to Trading Paints is governed by Trading
  Paints' terms.
